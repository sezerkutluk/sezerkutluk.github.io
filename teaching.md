## Lectures

### A Short Introduction to Model Calibration in Machine Learning
***Lecture*** | *within Doctoral Programme Bioeconomy, Leibniz Institute ATB (2024)*
* **Topics:** Trustworthiness in AI, probabilistic classifiers, Expected Calibration Error (ECE), overconfidence in deep neural networks, Platt scaling, and temperature scaling.
* [[View Lecture Slides PDF]](link-to-calibration-slides.pdf)

### Explainable AI for Large Language Models: Local Explanations
***Invited Guest Lecture*** | within *XAI PhD Course at Scuola Normale Superiore (2026)*
* **Topics:** Local attribution methods for encoder/decoder architectures, Integrated Gradients, SHAP/LIME, Inseq framework, attention as explanation debates, and RAG interpretability.
* **Scope & Style:** A *discussion- and nuance-oriented seminar* designed for doctoral researchers. Rather than repeating standard textbook definitions of basic attribution, the session focuses on critical open questions and recent methodological debates in LLM interpretability.
* [[View Lecture Slides PDF]](link-to-xai-llm-slides.pdf)
